Acme Corporation — Q3 2024 Internal Report

Financial Summary:
Acme Corporation reported quarterly revenue of $4.2 million in Q3 2024,
up 18% from Q2 2024. Net profit margin was 12.5%. The cloud services
division was the strongest performer, contributing $2.8 million in revenue.

Headcount:
The company employed 340 people at the end of Q3 2024, having hired 25 new
engineers during the quarter. The engineering team is led by CTO Maria Chen.

Product:
The flagship product, AcmeCloud, reached 12,000 active business customers.
A new AI-powered analytics feature launched in September 2024 and was adopted
by 30% of customers within the first month.

Outlook:
Management projects Q4 2024 revenue between $4.8 million and $5.1 million,
driven by continued growth in the cloud services division.
